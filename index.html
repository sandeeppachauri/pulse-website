Why Pulse Monitor

One screen for the entire middleware stack

Managing Enterprise Integration means juggling multiple consoles — WebLogic Admin, SOA Suite, OSB, EM — with no single view of health, faults, or deployments.

Pulse Monitor aggregates it all. Metric collection runs on a configurable schedule tuned to your environment and load. The scheduler analyzes logs automatically. Alerts fire before users notice problems.

Unified live dashboard
